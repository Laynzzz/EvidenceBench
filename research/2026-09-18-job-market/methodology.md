# Job-market review methodology

Research date: September 18, 2026 (America/New_York).

Requested sample: 200 distinct U.S. job opportunities, comprising 100 internships and 100 permanent full-time roles. Prioritize entry-level/new-graduate full-time opportunities. Label experienced roles separately. Sources: authenticated Handshake and LinkedIn job listings. This is a purposive convenience sample, not a probability sample or an estimate of the entire U.S. labor market.

Recency window: August 19–September 18, 2026. The user explicitly approved counting a recent repost as a recent posting, provided reposts are flagged. LinkedIn's past-month filter and the displayed posting/reposting age are captured together. Relative dates are not exact original publication dates. Handshake's detail-page posting age takes precedence over a card's generic 'New' label. Ambiguous '1 month ago' dates without a qualifying filter or more precise evidence are excluded.

Internships remain internships even if their working hours or a platform's employment label says full-time. Permanent jobs must have full-time employment evidence. Exclude unrelated software jobs, annotation/AI-rating gigs, non-U.S.-only jobs, stale postings, and records without a readable job description. AI software roles must involve building or operating AI functionality, not merely using an AI coding assistant.

Deduplication uses platform job IDs, employer/title comparisons, requisition codes where available, and review of substantially identical descriptions. Multi-location duplicates are one opportunity unless evidence establishes distinct requisitions. Cross-platform duplicates retain source provenance but count once. Employer concentration will be disclosed rather than hidden.

Requirements analysis uses employer-written descriptions. Handshake's AI-generated summaries and personalized qualification matches are not evidence of employer requirements. Skill mention frequencies are descriptive: absence of a term does not establish that a skill is unnecessary, and a mention does not by itself establish a mandatory qualification. Required versus preferred language, degree restrictions, and experience levels are retained where explicit.

The final assessment will compare model-focused MLE roles, AI application software roles, and ML infrastructure roles; internships and full-time jobs will be reported separately. PhD restrictions and experienced full-time positions will be labeled so they do not silently define the user's entry-level target.

Collection is read-only. No applications, messages, job alerts, saved jobs, or profile changes are authorized by this research request.
